• August 5, 1977

    Hired Cotton Fitzsimmons as Head Coach.

    Don Adams signed as a veteran free agent with the Detroit Pistons; received a 1978 3rd round draft pick (Ricky Gallon was later selected) as compensation.

  • September 1, 1977

    Traded Mike Bantom and Adrian Dantley to the Indiana Pacers for Billy Knight.

    Traded George Johnson, a 1978 1st round draft pick (Micheal Ray Richardson was later selected) and a 1979 1st round draft pick (Cliff Robinson was later selected) to the New Jersey Nets for Tiny Archibald.

    Signed Mike Bantom as a veteran free agent and sent a future 4th round draft pick to the New Jersey Nets as compensation.

  • September 7, 1977

    Traded Ernie DiGregorio to the Los Angeles Lakers for future considerations.

  • June 12, 1978

    Mike Glenn signed as a veteran free agent with the New York Knicks; received FROM_TRADE as compensation.
