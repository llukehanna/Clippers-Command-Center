• July 15, 1978

    Received a 1979 2nd round draft pick (Gary Garland was later selected) as compensation for the Golden State Warriors signing Tom Abernethy as a veteran free agent.

  • May 13, 1979

    Signed Bill Walton as a veteran free agent and sent Kevin Kunnert, Kermit Washington and a 1980 1st round draft pick (Mike Gminski was later selected) to the Portland Trail Blazers as compensation.
